The Signal vs. Narrative Test

Before any transformation program: four questions that separate credible value from narrative.

1. Is there a verifiable baseline?
Can the vendor show current-state performance against a frozen baseline? "We'll establish that in Phase 1" means the savings have no anchor.
2. Is the savings mechanism specific?
Does it explain exactly which claim type, member segment, or workflow decision changes — and by how much? Vague "reducing unnecessary utilization" is not a mechanism.
3. Who bears implementation risk?
If the engagement is fee-for-service and savings projections are vendor-supplied, the vendor has no skin in the game. Value-based contracts are the credible structure.
4. What's the real counterfactual?
ROI vs. "doing nothing" is structurally favorable. The real test is: what else could this budget achieve? Every investment should be benchmarked against its best alternative.
